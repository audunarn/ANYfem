# NON-OPERATIVE / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Sample ANYfem commercial license term sheet — discussion draft

**This document is not an offer, contract or license grant. No permission arises
from download, use, contribution, enquiry or payment discussions. All fields and
clauses require legal review and an executed agreement. Public rights continue
under the applicable existing open-source licenses.**

| Topic | Matters for negotiation and legal drafting |
|---|---|
| Parties and authority | Identify licensor, licensee, signing authority and chain-of-title evidence; do not assume all repository material is licensor-owned |
| Licensed material | Exact source-file schedule, exclusions, commit/tag and version; separate binaries, docs, data and trademarks |
| Proposed grant | Define reproduction, modification, proprietary integration and redistribution rights for eligible material only; specify territory, duration and deployment model |
| OEM/downstream scope | Named products, affiliates, end users, sublicensing, source access, redistribution notices and restrictions; distinguish end-user use from reseller rights |
| Third-party schedule | Exact component versions, licenses, notices, source access and replacement/relinking duties; no sublicense beyond rights held; Qt commercial licensing if separately required |
| Consideration | Pricing/payment/taxes to be negotiated; no price or entitlement established by this draft |
| Version and updates | Covered release/commit range, patch and upgrade entitlement, contributor clearance for future versions, maintenance renewal and end of support |
| Support | Optional separate statement of work, platforms, response targets, exclusions and acceptance tests; no implied unlimited support |
| Modifications | Ownership of customer changes, upstream contribution rights and any negotiated grant back; no presumed assignment |
| Engineering responsibility | Customer analysis responsibility, agreed validation scope, no implied qualification, regulatory certification or fitness warranty |
| Risk allocation | Counsel to draft any warranties, disclaimers, liability limits, indemnities and insurance; this draft promises none |
| Confidentiality / data | Separate confidentiality terms and treatment of proprietary models, security reports and personal data |
| Compliance / termination | Evidence and cure process, effects on previously shipped units, survival and treatment of public GPL rights; no retroactive revocation of earlier GPL/MPL grants |
| General terms | Governing law, forum, dispute resolution, assignment, export/other applicable obligations and signatures, all subject to counsel |

Negotiation prerequisites: eligible-source register, contribution permission
review, version-specific third-party/binary inventory, actual deployment route,
commercial and support scope, legal approval. Maintainer release approval and a
separate signed agreement are required before delivering a commercial grant.
