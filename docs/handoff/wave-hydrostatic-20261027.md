## Design as implemented
- `include_hydrostatic: bool=False` is the trailing field of `RegularWaveInput` and `IrregularWaveInput`; strict `type is bool`, absent in old data = False.
- New `application/wave_hydrostatic.py`: `EXPRESSION='1025.0*9.81*max(1.5-z,0.0)'` (project z, waterline z=1.5; 15082.875 Pa at z=0, zero at/above 1.5), `make_static_pressure(dynamic)` (ordinary `WetPressure` expression, same surfaces, scale 1, no envelope, id = UUID5 of the owning dynamic id), `is_owned_static`/`owned_pair`/`wave_loads_without_companion` (exact-attribute ownership checks), `history_includes_hydrostatic` (provenance `settings`, missing=False, non-bool refused), `source_baseline()` (ANYhydro public `buoyancy_baseline_wrench` on `_source_mesh()` about WORLD0; expected `(0,0,4524862.5,0,-135745875,0)`), `total_resultants`, `composition`.
- `wave_force_input.py`: `_same_inputs` ignores `name` and `include_hydrostatic` (retained dynamic reuse, no BEM/recovery on toggle). Preview always starts from dynamic resultants, adds the baseline once, stores `preview_composition` and `preview_hydrostatic_baseline`, binds them (only when enabled, so off-hashes are unchanged) in `preview_content_hash`; `validate_force_preview` recomputes the baseline and refuses tampered/inconsistent previews. Saved SourceAware force payload stays a dynamic bank.
- `ApplyWavePressure.do`: `mixed()` sets aside only a validated owned companion; any other extra wet pressure, gravity, envelope, scale, parameters still refuse replacement. Appends one static companion after the dynamic `WetPressure` iff provenance flag is True. Rollback/undo/redo unchanged (before/after maps).
- `solve/build.py` `_BatchLoadContext`: registry of owned companions found once at construction (`id(load)` → (load, dynamic)); `note_load` trusts such a load after cheap re-validation only. Arbitrary expressions/subclasses/unregistered look-alikes still flush as before.
- Qt: `WaveLoadDialog.hydrostatic` checkbox (`wave_include_hydrostatic`) with note (draft 1.5 m, rho 1025, g 9.81, nothing else added); in `settings()`, restored from saved metadata, reset in `start_new_record`, disabled with other controls during runs, toggle = `invalidate_preview('include_hydrostatic')` that keeps retained record + chosen interval like a rename; retained-reuse comparison ignores the flag. `WaveResultantPlot.set_composition()` sets the title (“… (dynamic-only)” or “… + steady hydrostatic buoyancy”); `record_info` also states the composition.
